# The Inward Ear

Most attention points outward. Interoception is the sense that points in
— the felt pulse, the breath, the gut, the quiet body-signals that
underwrite emotion and the bare feeling of being alive. Tune into it and
the texture of experience changes.

Make a piece played by attending inward instead of out. It amplifies a
body — a heartbeat, a breath cycle — and asks the player to sync with it,
follow it, notice it: match a slow tempo, ride the in-and-out, hold
attention on a signal that keeps trying to slip below notice. Reward
stillness and listening, not reflex. It can be closer to a calming
instrument than an arcade game, and that's right for this one.

Sound is the body made audible — the whole experience is a duet with a
pulse you learn to hear. Consider the device microphone or motion sensor
only if it stays trivial and permissioned; an imagined body works fine
and keeps it universal.

## Constraints
- Playable in seconds: breathe with it, listen in. No manual.
- Sound *is* the interior — heartbeat/breath as the instrument. Audio off
  empties it. Web Audio, local; no CDN.
- Mobile-web first: portrait, touch, opens cold in a phone browser, no
  build, no server, no login. This page is its own door.
- One self-contained page under `growth/`; vendor any assets.

## Bloom looks like
- The player gets quiet, hears an inner rhythm, and feels their own
  aliveness turned up a notch.
