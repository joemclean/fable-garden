# The Width of Now

The present moment isn't a knife-edge point; it has width. The "specious
present" is the little window — a fraction of a second to a couple of
seconds — that consciousness holds as *now*, within which events feel
simultaneous or flowing rather than remembered. Change the width and you
change what "now" contains.

Make a rhythm-toy where the player can stretch and shrink the window of
the present. Inside the window, events bind into one felt moment — a
chord, a gesture, a flow; outside it, they fall into before-and-after.
Widen it and far-apart beats fuse into a single now; narrow it and one now
shatters into a sequence. The play is composing with the size of the
moment: making simultaneity, making flow, making staccato time.

Sound is the medium of nowness — simultaneity and succession are heard
before they're seen. Let the player *feel* the window resize in their ear,
notes clumping into a present or scattering into a line.

## Constraints
- Playable in seconds: tap the pulse, stretch the moment. No manual.
- Sound is where the present has width — rhythm and simultaneity are the
  mechanic. Web Audio, local; no CDN.
- Mobile-web first: portrait, touch, opens cold in a phone browser, no
  build, no server, no login. This page is its own door.
- One self-contained page under `growth/`; vendor any assets.

## Bloom looks like
- The player feels "now" change size — beats fusing into a moment or
  splitting into a line — and plays with time's grain.
