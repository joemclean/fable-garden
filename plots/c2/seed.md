# The Guessing Machine

You don't see the world; you see your brain's best guess about the world,
corrected here and there by the parts that surprise it. Perception is a
controlled hallucination — top-down prediction meeting bottom-up error.
Most of what you experience, you supplied.

Make a world that renders from the player's *prediction*, not from raw
truth. Show them a pattern; they come to expect it; the screen and the
sound fill in from that expectation. Where reality matches, all is calm
and quiet. Where it deviates, a prediction-error flares — a visual jolt,
a stab of dissonance — and the model updates. The game is living inside
your own predictions and feeling the jolt when the world refuses them.

Audio predicts too: a phrase the ear completes, a resolution it expects.
Withhold or bend it and the miss should sting before the eye even catches
up. Let the player learn to *use* prediction — and be punished by
over-confidence, the way a strong hallucination overrides a weak signal.

## Constraints
- Playable in seconds: fall into the pattern, feel it break. No manual.
- Sound is a prediction channel — dissonance is the error signal, not
  ornament. Web Audio, local; no CDN.
- Mobile-web first: portrait, touch, opens cold in a phone browser, no
  build, no server, no login. This page is its own door.
- One self-contained page under `growth/`; vendor any assets.

## Bloom looks like
- The player notices they've been *supplying* the world, and the
  prediction-error jolt becomes something they can feel coming.
