# Below the Line

Most of what steers you never reaches you. A face flashed too fast to
see, a word too quiet to hear, still tilts the next choice you'd swear
was freely yours. The unconscious votes first and lets consciousness take
the credit.

Build something that nudges the player below the threshold of notice,
then shows them the strings. Let them make a run of free-feeling choices
— pick a door, a color, a word — while cues sit just under the line: a
shape flashed for a few frames, a whisper mixed almost inaudibly, a
rhythm that primes a tempo. Track how the hidden cues bend the choices.
Then the turn: replay it with the subliminal layer made visible and
audible, so the player watches their own "free" run get puppeted.

Handle it honestly — the reveal is the whole point, and it should feel
like a magic trick explained, not a manipulation hidden. Audio is a prime
channel for the subliminal: sound sits under awareness better than image.

## Constraints
- Playable in seconds: just start choosing. The reveal comes after.
- Sound carries subliminal primes and the reveal — integral, not decor.
  Web Audio, local; no CDN.
- Mobile-web first: portrait, touch, opens cold in a phone browser, no
  build, no server, no login. This page is its own door.
- One self-contained page under `growth/`; vendor any assets.

## Bloom looks like
- The player sees their free choices were nudged, feels the floor tilt —
  then wants to test it on a friend.
