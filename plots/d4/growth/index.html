CATCH YOURSELF

Tap anywhere, once, on each swell of the orb. That is the whole task.

It will get boring. Your mind will leave — it always does. The game is noticing the moment you realize you've been gone: press the band at the bottom the instant you catch yourself.

Sound on, if you can. The drone stays true while you're here and slides while you're away — hearing it slip and catching yourself are the same act.
