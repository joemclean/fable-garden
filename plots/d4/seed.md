# Catch Yourself

You can attend, and you can attend to your attending. Metacognition is the
mind watching itself — and its most humbling demonstration is
mind-wandering: you set out to focus, drift without noticing, and only
sometime later *catch yourself* having been gone. The catching is the
skill.

Build a game whose real task is noticing the moment your own attention
slips. Give the player something simple to hold focus on — a pulse to
follow, a thing to track — but score the *catch*: the instant they realize
their mind wandered, they mark it, and that self-noticing is what's
rewarded, not the surface task. Make the drift genuine and tempting, not
just a distractor to click.

Audio is the mirror of attention: while the player holds focus the sound
stays true, and as they drift it subtly slides — out of tune, off tempo,
thinning — so that catching the drift and catching the detuning are the
same act. The uncanny bit: realizing, mid-play, that you'd been gone a
while and hadn't known.

## Constraints
- Playable in seconds: hold focus, notice when you've lost it, mark the
  catch. Minimal manual.
- Sound is the drift-meter — it detunes as attention wanders; audio off
  removes the mirror. Web Audio, local; no CDN.
- Mobile-web first: portrait, touch, opens cold in a phone browser, no
  build, no server, no login. This page is its own door.
- One self-contained page under `growth/`; vendor any assets.

## Bloom looks like
- The player catches themselves having drifted — hears it and feels it at
  once — and gets a little better at the catching.
