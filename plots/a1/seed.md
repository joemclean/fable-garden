# The Narrow Beam

Attention is a spotlight, not a floodlight. We feel like we take in a
whole scene at once, but awareness is a narrow moving cone and the rest
is dark — present, unattended, unheard.

Make a small world that only resolves where the player points. A finger
drags a soft-edged beam across a dim screen; inside it, things sharpen
into focus and their sounds rise; outside it, shapes stay vague and their
sounds sink almost to nothing. The whole soundscape is always playing at
full — birds, a voice, a machine, a bell — but the player only *hears*
what the beam is on, so sweeping it around is a constant small discovery
of things that were there the entire time.

Somewhere in here is a game, not just a toy: maybe something happens only
when unobserved, so the player has to learn what they lose by looking.
Let the mechanic teach the idea in the hand, not in text.

## Constraints
- Playable in seconds: point the beam, hear the world change. No manual.
- Sound is the mechanic. With audio off the piece is empty — the beam's
  whole job is selecting what you hear. Web Audio, generated or vendored
  locally; no CDN.
- Mobile-web first: portrait, touch, opens cold in a phone browser, no
  build, no server, no login. This page is its own door.
- One self-contained page under `growth/`; vendor any assets.

## Bloom looks like
- A stranger picks it up on a phone, sweeps the beam, and *gets it* —
  feels the darkness around their own attention — without being told.
