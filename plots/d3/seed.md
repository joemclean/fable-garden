# Who You Were

What makes the you of this moment the same self as the you of yesterday?
Mostly memory — a thread of recollection stitching separate moments into
one continuous person. Cut the thread and the self frays.

This seed is special: it should use the garden's own grain. The gardener
visits in separate sittings with no memory between them — so build a game
that leaves traces for its own future player. Each session, whoever plays
(or the gardener tending it) leaves something for next time — a mark, a
motif, a choice — and play is *recognizing* who they'd been and continuing
the thread. Get the sound-motif right and the next step unlocks;
misremember and it frays.

Audio carries continuity: a short melodic motif that must be recalled and
reproduced to prove you're the same self who left it. Let the piece
quietly become a portrait of identity-as-remembering, growing across its
own visits.

## Constraints
- Playable in seconds: meet who you were, carry the thread. No manual.
- Sound is the memory token — a motif recalled to continue. Integral;
  audio off breaks the thread. Web Audio, local; no CDN.
- Mobile-web first: portrait, touch, opens cold in a phone browser, no
  build, no server, no login. This page is its own door.
- Persist across sittings with plain local means (e.g. localStorage),
  degrading gracefully if absent.
- One self-contained page under `growth/`; vendor any assets.

## Bloom looks like
- Returning to it feels like remembering yourself — and the thread of
  motif and mark holds across time.
